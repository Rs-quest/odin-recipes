Kapsalon

a dutch street food classic

Prep Time

15 minutes

Cook Time

30 minutes

Servings

4 people

Ingredients

  • French fries
  • Döner meat or shawarma
  • Gouda cheese
  • Lettuce
  • Tomatoes
  • Garlic sauce
  • Hot sauce (optional)

Instructions

  1. Prepare the fries according to package instructions
  2. Cook the döner meat until crispy
  3. Layer fries in an oven-safe dish
  4. Top with meat and grated cheese
  5. Broil until cheese melts
  6. Top with fresh lettuce and tomatoes
  7. Drizzle with garlic sauce and hot sauce
  8. Enjoy while hot!
