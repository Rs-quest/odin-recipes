Fluffy Pancakes

breakfast perfection

Prep Time

5 minutes

Cook Time

15 minutes

Servings

4 people

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp white sugar
  • 1 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp butter, melted

Instructions

  1. Mix flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar in a bowl
  2. Make a well in the center
  3. Pour in milk, egg, and melted butter
  4. Mix until smooth (small lumps are okay)
  5. Heat griddle or pan over medium-high heat
  6. Pour 1/4 cup batter for each pancake
  7. Cook until bubbles form, then flip
  8. Cook until golden brown on both sides
  9. Serve with maple syrup and butter!
